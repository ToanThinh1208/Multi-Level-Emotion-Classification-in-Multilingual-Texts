# Multilingual Emotion Classification via Star-Based Product Review Analysis
## FPT University, Ho Chi Minh Campus, Vietnam

In e-commerce, star ratings play an important role, but sometimes they do not accurately reflect customers’ actual
feelings. The study proposed emotional intensity classification models based on customer product ratings from 1 to
5 stars. The study combines Vietnamese and English to provide a more accurate perspective for manufacturers
We compared traditional machine learning models such as Na¨ıve Bayes, SVM, XGBoost and mBERT deep
learning model on Kaggle dataset. The results showed that SVM achieved the highest accuracy in the range of
77-78% but mBERT classified better at 3 and 4 stars. However, both models are affected by imbalanced data.
The research aims to optimize the model and expand methods to resolve data imbalance in the future
